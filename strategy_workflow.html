1H Auto-Range  |  15M BOS  |  5M Rejection Candle Entry  |  0.5% Risk Per Trade

① Range — Set Automatically
How the range is set
Last 20 hours of 1H bars
Range High = highest 1H bar high in the last 20 hours
Range Low  = lowest 1H bar low in the last 20 hours
Mid        = midpoint between high and low

No manual input needed. Updates automatically when new 1H structure forms.
What you see on the chart
3 flat horizontal lines
─── Range High (red)
- - - Mid (gray dashed)
─── Range Low (green)

Info table top-right shows all three values live.
② Price Must Enter a Boundary Zone
For a SHORT setup
Price must touch the HIGH zone
Within 8% of Range High
Chart background turns red when in zone
Info table shows: AT LEVEL — HIGH ZONE
For a LONG setup
Price must touch the LOW zone
Within 8% of Range Low
Chart background turns green when in zone
Info table shows: AT LEVEL — LOW ZONE
③ 15M Break of Structure (BOS)
SHORT — After touching HIGH zone
15M candle closes below a 15M pivot low
That broken pivot low becomes the BOS level
Chart shows: 15M BOS ▼ label
Info table: 15M BOS State — BEAR ACTIVE
LONG — After touching LOW zone
15M candle closes above a 15M pivot high
That broken pivot high becomes the BOS level
Chart shows: 15M BOS ▲ label
Info table: 15M BOS State — BULL ACTIVE
④ Filters — All Must Pass Before Entry
R:R ≥ 1.5
Distance from entry to Mid (TP1) must be at least 1.5× the risk to SL.
If too close to mid — no trade.
ATR Filter
5M ATR(14) must be above its 50-bar average.
Blocks entries during flat / low-volatility conditions.
Range Break Filter
If price closes more than 15% outside the range boundary — entries pause until range updates to new structure.
One Trade at a Time
No new entry if a position is already open.
Pyramiding is off.

▼ SHORT Entry

MeaningPrice retested the broken level
and got rejected back down
SLBOS level + ATR × 0.5

▲ LONG Entry
Trigger5M low touches BOS level
AND 5M closes above it
MeaningPrice retested the broken level
and bounced back up
SLBOS level − ATR × 0.5
TP1Range Mid (close 50% here)
TP2Range High
Risk0.5% of account ($500 on $100k)
⑥ Trade Management
TP1 Hits (Mid)
Close 50% of the position.
Remaining half stays open.
Breakeven logic triggers immediately.
Breakeven
SL moves to entry price.
Remaining 50% now runs to TP2 with zero risk.
Stop Loss Hit
Full position closed.
Loss = 0.5% of account.
Rotation gate re-arms for next setup.
TP2 Hit
Remaining 50% closes at opposite boundary.
Win recorded. Rotation gate resets.
⑦ Rotation Gate (Optional — on by default)
Prevents taking the same side back-to-back
After a SHORT fires → must wait for price to reach the LOW zone before another entry is allowed
After a LONG fires → must wait for price to reach the HIGH zone

Info table shows: Armed Side — WAIT→LOW or WAIT→HIGH
Once price reaches that zone it resets to BOTH and trading resumes

Can be turned off in the script Filters settings.
